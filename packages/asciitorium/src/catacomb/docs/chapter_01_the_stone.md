# Chapter 1 — The Stone

## Knowledge Drop

The journey took eleven days.

You stand before it now. A wall of weathered stone rising from the earth at an unnatural angle, as though something vast was buried beneath it and this was the only part that refused to stay underground.

You step closer. Your fingers brush across the surface to find grooves. The stone is cold, but you find carvings. Your father's lessons come back to you, fragments of a dead language pieced together from a lifetime of obsession. You recognize a symbol here. A word there. Enough to read a few broken pieces of a sentence that has waited centuries for someone to find:

*"Welcome tothe Cantus… anchor of… instrument of…"*

Its all you can make out. 

And then you hear it. Faint. Miserable.

A hum, in a strange key. Coming from somewhere far below, rising up through the stone beneath your feet.

You lean forward, pressing your ear to the carvings, and the stone shifts. A crack splits the earth. The ground gives way beneath you, and you fall, light shrinking to a point above you, the surface world vanishing in an instant.

You hit the ground hard. The air is different down here — heavier, older, thick with something you cannot name.

And then, from somewhere deeper in the dark, the melody returns. Louder now. Closer. Wrong in a way that makes your chest ache.

It almost sounds like it is calling you.

## Level Design — Chapter 1: The Stone

### Overview
The player's introduction to the game. This level teaches core movement and interaction mechanics in a high-tension, low-danger environment. The player has fallen into the outermost reaches of the Cantus with nothing — no map, no light, no sense of direction. The level is small and contained, designed to orient the player without overwhelming them.

### Starting Conditions
- **No light.** The screen is completely dark. The player can hear ambient sound — dripping water — but can see nothing.
- **No map.** The player has no minimap or mapping ability. They are lost.
- **No directional indicators.** No compass, no waypoint, no UI guidance. The player must explore by feel.

### Progression

**Step 1 — The Torch**
The player's first input. Any movement forward causes the hero to step on something. A prompt appears: *"You feel something underfoot."* The player picks it up (E key). It is a torch. It lights automatically — the screen floods with light, revealing a small stone chamber. This is the player's first moment of relief and their introduction to the pickup mechanic.

**Step 2 — Exploration**
With the torch lit, the player can now see the immediate area — a rough chamber with a few branching passages. This section teaches:
- **Movement (WASD keys)** — navigating tight corridors, turning corners, dead ends
- **Item pickup (E key)** — scattered items to collect:
  - **Bone** — found on the ground near what appears to be old remains. Will be useful later for taming.
  - **Cloth** — torn fabric snagged on a jagged wall. Basic crafting/utility material.
  - Other minor items that establish the habit of searching the environment.
- **Environmental awareness** — the player should notice the carvings on the walls (echoing the carvings from the surface), and the sense that this place was *built*, not natural. These are atmospheric details, not interactive yet — but they begin training the player to pay attention to the Cantus's architecture.

**Step 3 — Finding the Way Down**
The chamber has a few short passages to explore, some leading to dead ends with minor loot, one leading to a staircase descending further into the Cantus. The staircase is the exit to Chapter 2.

### Design Goals
- **Teach controls in context.** No tutorial screen. The player learns WASD and E by necessity — they need to move and they need to pick things up.
- **Establish tone.** The darkness, the silence, the slow reveal of light — this sets the mood for the entire game. The Cantus is not a friendly place, but it is not mindlessly hostile either. It is old, broken, and waiting.
- **Train attention to sound.** The melody getting louder near the correct path is the first instance of the game's core principle: listen to the Cantus. This becomes critical as wish songs and curse songs are introduced in later levels.
- **Keep it short.** This level should take 3-5 minutes. It is a controlled introduction, not a full dungeon. The player should feel oriented and curious by the time they reach the stairs, not exhausted.

### Items Found
| Item | Location | Purpose |
|------|----------|---------|
| Torch | Directly underfoot at start | Provides light; first pickup |
| Bone | Near old remains in side passage | Taming material (used in later levels) |
| Cloth | Snagged on wall in dead-end passage | Crafting/utility material for bandages or torches |

### Audio Notes
- **Pre-torch:** Near silence. Dripping water.
- **Post-torch:** Ambient stone sounds. Faint echoes of the player's footsteps — the Cantus's acoustics are already at work.
