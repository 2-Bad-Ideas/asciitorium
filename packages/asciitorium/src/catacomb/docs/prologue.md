# Catacomb: The Tomb of Tahara — Prologue

As a child of the Ashwild tribe, survival always came first. Your people were scavengers — nomads who roamed the broken lands, living off what the world left behind. You learned to track before you could read. You learned to hide before you could fight. The wilds were not kind, and the Ashwild did not expect them to be.

But your tribe had something the other scavenger clans did not. A tradition passed down through generations, as old as the tribe itself — the ability to tame wild creatures. You grew up alongside wolves that answered to a whistle. You learned to calm a snarling beast with nothing but stillness and an open hand. Your mother taught you that taming was not about breaking a creature's will. It was about earning its trust. Patience. Offering. Respect.

Your father taught you something different. While the rest of the tribe focused on the survival of the tribe your father looked to the past. He collected fragments of an old language no one else cared about. He traced carvings on ruins the other scavengers ignored. And at night, by the fire, he hummed.

Strange melodies. He said they were echoes of a forgotten age — scraps of something called wish songs that once had the power to shape the world. He said there was a place, deep beneath the earth, where the music still lived. He said the land had not always been broken. That once, it sang.

When the time came to choose your trial — the solo journey into the wilds that every young scavenger must survive to come of age — you chose the forbidden place. The ancient site your father spoke of in his song, the one the elders warned was cursed...
